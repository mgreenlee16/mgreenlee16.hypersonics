Normal Shock

Enter any one quantity to get the upstream Mach number and every ratio across a normal shock, in place of the normal-shock table (NACA 1135, Table II; Anderson, Appendix B).

Relations used

$$M_2^2 = \frac{1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_1^2}{\gamma M_1^2-\frac{\gamma-1}{2}},\quad \frac{p_2}{p_1} = 1+\frac{2\gamma}{\gamma+1}(M_1^2-1),\quad \frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1} = \frac{(\gamma+1)M_1^2}{2+(\gamma-1)M_1^2}$$

The Pitot ratio p₀₂/p₁ is the Rayleigh Pitot-tube formula. Every ratio is monotonic in M₁, so any one fixes the shock. See Lesson 2.
