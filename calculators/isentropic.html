Isentropic Flow

Enter any one quantity to get the Mach number and every isentropic ratio, in place of the isentropic-flow table (NACA 1135, Table I; Anderson, Appendix A).

Relations used

$$\frac{T_0}{T} = 1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2,\quad \frac{p_0}{p} = \left(\frac{T_0}{T}\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}},\quad \frac{\rho_0}{\rho} = \left(\frac{T_0}{T}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}},\quad \frac{A}{A^*} = \frac{1}{M}\left[\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\frac{T_0}{T}\right]^{\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)}}$$

Area ratio and Prandtl–Meyer inputs are inverted by bisection. Area ratio has a subsonic and a supersonic root, so pick the branch. See Lesson 1 and Lesson 3.
