Fanno Flow

Adiabatic, constant-area flow with wall friction. Get 4f̄L*/D and the starred ratios from any one quantity, or the exit state for a given duct length.

Process: inlet M₁ and duct length

Relations used

$$\frac{4\bar fL^*}{D} = \frac{1-M^2}{\gamma M^2} + \frac{\gamma+1}{2\gamma}\ln\frac{(\gamma+1)M^2}{2+(\gamma-1)M^2},\quad \frac{T}{T^*} = \frac{\gamma+1}{2+(\gamma-1)M^2},\quad \frac{p}{p^*} = \frac{1}{M}\sqrt{\frac{T}{T^*}}$$

f̄ is the Fanning friction coefficient (one quarter of the Darcy factor). See Lesson 4.
