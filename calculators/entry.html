Entry Trajectory

Planar three-degree-of-freedom entry over a spherical, non-rotating Earth, with constant L/D and bank angle, the 1976 Standard Atmosphere to 150 km, and Sutton–Graves stagnation heating. For a ballistic entry, the Allen–Eggers closed form is shown alongside.

Relations used

\(\dot V = -\rho V^2/(2\beta) - g\sin\gamma\), \(V\dot\gamma = (L/D)\rho V^2\cos\sigma/(2\beta) - (g - V^2/r)\cos\gamma\), \(\dot h = V\sin\gamma\), with β = m/(C_DA) and g = μ/r², integrated by fourth-order Runge–Kutta. Heating \(q = 1.7415\times10^{-4}\sqrt{\rho/R_n}V^3\) (cold wall, convective only); heat load by trapezoidal integration. Allen–Eggers: \(a_\max = V_E^2\sin\gamma_E/(2eH)\), H = 7.2 km. A run is flagged as a skip-out if the vehicle climbs back above its entry altitude. See Lesson 13.
