Newtonian Aerodynamics

Modified-Newtonian force and moment coefficients for capsules, blunted cones, sharp cones, spheres and flat plates at any angle of attack, with trim and static stability about a chosen center of gravity. Reference area: base area πD²/4; reference length: diameter D.

ShapesFor a capsule, δ is the afterbody half-angle (closing toward the apex, shadowed at moderate α). For a sphere-cone or sharp cone, δ is the forebody half-angle. The γ in the top bar sets Cp,max.

Relations used

Each windward panel (\(\hat{\mathbf V}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n} < 0\)) of the body of revolution, divided into 96 azimuthal strips, carries \(C_p = C_{p,\max}(\hat{\mathbf V}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n})^2\), with \(C_{p,\max}\) from the Rayleigh–Pitot relation; shadowed panels carry \(C_p = 0\). Forces and the pitching moment about the CG (nose-up positive) are summed over panels. Trim is where \(C_{m,cg} = 0\); stable if \(\partial C_m/\partial\alpha < 0\). Positive α puts the lower surface windward. See Lesson 10.
