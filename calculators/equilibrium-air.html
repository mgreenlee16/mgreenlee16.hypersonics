Normal shock in equilibrium air

Relations used

Equilibrium follows the law of mass action for every species formed from N and O atoms and electrons, with charge neutrality and a fixed N:O ratio (79% N₂, 21% O₂ by volume), solved by Newton's method in logarithmic variables. Thermodynamic functions: McBride, Zehe & Gordon, NASA TP-2002-211556 (standard state 1 bar). The solver reproduces Cantera's equilibrium to a few parts per million. The shock uses \(p_2 = p_1 + \rho_1u_1^2(1-\varepsilon)\), \(h_2 = h_1 + \tfrac12u_1^2(1-\varepsilon^2)\), iterated on \(\varepsilon = \rho_1/\rho_2\). The stagnation state behind it is reached isentropically in equilibrium. The effective γ̃ = h/e uses enthalpy measured from cold air at 0 K. See Lesson 11.
