Ramjet & Scramjet Cycle

Station-by-station Brayton-cycle analysis after Heiser and Pratt, with compression, burner and expansion efficiencies. Specify the inlet either by its cycle temperature ratio ψ = T₃/T₀ or by the burner-entry Mach number M₃. M₃ below 1 is a ramjet; above 1, a scramjet.

Relations used

\(V_3^2 = V_0^2 - 2c_p(T_3 - T_0)\); \(p_3/p_0 = [\psi/(\psi(1-\eta_c)+\eta_c)]^{c_p/R}\); constant-pressure burner with fuel injected without axial momentum, \(V_4 = V_3/(1+f)\), \((1+f)(c_{pb}T_4 + V_4^2/2) = c_pT_3 + V_3^2/2 + \eta_bfh_{PR}\); nozzle to ambient pressure, \(T_{10} = T_4\{1 - \eta_e[1 - (p_0/p_3)^{R/c_{pb}}]\}\); \(F/\dot m_0 = (1+f)V_{10} - V_0\). Heating values: H₂ 119.95 MJ/kg (f_st = 0.0292), hydrocarbon 43.9 MJ/kg (f_st = 0.0676). Constant specific heats overestimate T₄ when it exceeds about 2,500 K. See Lesson 14.
