Oblique Shock

Any two of upstream Mach number, deflection angle θ and wave angle β give the third, for the weak or strong solution, along with every ratio across the shock.

Relations used

$$\tan\theta = 2\cot\beta\,\frac{M_1^2\sin^2\beta-1}{M_1^2(\gamma+\cos2\beta)+2},\qquad M_1^2 = \frac{2(\cot\beta+\tan\theta)}{\sin2\beta-\tan\theta(\gamma+\cos2\beta)}$$

Given M₁ and θ, β is found by bisection on the weak (μ < β < βmax) or strong (βmax < β < 90°) branch. If θ > θmax the shock is detached. Downstream properties use the normal-shock relations with Mn1 = M₁ sin β, and M₂ = Mn2/sin(β − θ). See Lesson 5.
