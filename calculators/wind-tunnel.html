Supersonic Wind Tunnel

Size a supersonic tunnel and estimate its operating envelope: test-section conditions, nozzle throat, mass flow, diffuser second throat, starting and running pressure ratios, compressor power, blowdown run time, and the humidity limit for condensation.

Relations used

Test conditions from the isentropic relations; \(\dot m = 0.0404\,p_0A_{t1}/\sqrt{T_0}\) for air; \(A_{t1} = A_\text{test}/(A/A^*)\); second throat \(A_{t2}/A_{t1} \ge p_{0,1}/p_{0,2}\) at the test Mach number; starting ratio \(p_0/p_b \approx p_{0,1}/p_{0,2}(M_t)\) and running ratio \(p_{0,1}/p_{0,2}(M_{t2})\); ideal compressor power \(\dot mc_pT_0[(p_0/p_b)^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}-1]\); isothermal blowdown run time \(V(p_i - 1.1p_0)/(RT_0\dot m)\). Viscosity from Sutherland's law. See Lesson 8.
