Aeroheating

Stagnation-point heating of a blunt nose (Sutton–Graves) and its radiation-adiabatic wall temperature, and laminar and turbulent heating along a flat plate at incidence or a sharp cone by Eckert's reference-temperature method, at any flight condition.

Stagnation point

Plate or cone

Relations used

Stagnation: \(q_{cw} = 1.7415\times10^{-4}\sqrt{\rho_\infty/R_n}\,V^3\) W/m², hot-wall factor \(1 - h_w/h_0\), radiation-adiabatic \(\varepsilon\sigma T_w^4 = q_{cw}(1 - h_w/h_0)\). Plate and cone: edge state from the oblique-shock or Taylor–Maccoll solution; \(T^*/T_e = 1 + 0.032M_e^2 + 0.58(T_w/T_e - 1)\); \(C_f^* = 0.664/\sqrt{Re^*_x}\) (laminar) or \(0.0592/(Re^*_x)^{0.2}\) (turbulent); Mangler factor √3 (laminar) or 1.15 (turbulent) for cones; \(St = \tfrac12C_f^*Pr^{-2/3}\), Pr = 0.71. The laminar thickness comes from the Blasius–Crocco profile in Dorodnitsyn variables, and the second-mode frequency estimate is \(u_e/2\delta\). See Lesson 12.
