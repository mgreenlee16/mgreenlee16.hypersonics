Prandtl–Meyer Function

The Prandtl–Meyer function and Mach angle for one Mach number, and the flow after a turn: an expansion around a convex corner, or a gradual isentropic compression.

Flow after a turn

Relations used

$$\nu(M) = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}\tan^{-1}\sqrt{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma+1}(M^2-1)} - \tan^{-1}\sqrt{M^2-1},\qquad \nu(M_2) = \nu(M_1)\pm\theta$$

Plus for expansion, minus for isentropic compression. Pressure, temperature and density ratios follow from the isentropic relations, since p₀ and T₀ are constant. See Lesson 6.
