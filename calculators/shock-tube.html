Relations used

Shock tube: the shock-tube equation for p₄/p₁ solved for p₂/p₁; reflected shock from $$\frac{M_R}{M_R^2-1} = \frac{M_s}{M_s^2-1}\sqrt{1 + \frac{2(\gamma-1)}{(\gamma+1)^2}(M_s^2-1)\left(\gamma + \frac{1}{M_s^2}\right)}.$$ Real-gas effects lower T₅ when it exceeds about 2,500 K. Ludwieg tube: tube Mach from the subsonic area–Mach relation for tube/throat area; unsteady expansion \(a/a_i = 1/(1 + \tfrac{\gamma-1}{2}M_t)\); cycle time \(L/a_i + L/(u+a)\). Liquefaction: N₂ saturation curve (Lesson 15). See Lesson 15.
