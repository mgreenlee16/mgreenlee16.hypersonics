Relations used

The Taylor–Maccoll equation is integrated with fourth-order Runge–Kutta from the shock, where the oblique-shock relations give the starting state, to the surface where \(V_\theta = 0\). The shock angle for a given cone angle is found by bisection on the weak branch, below the maximum cone angle. Surface conditions follow from the isentropic compression between shock and cone. Modified Newtonian: \(C_p = C_{p,\max}\sin^2\delta\). See Lesson 10; results match the NACA 1135 cone charts (for example, M = 2, δ = 10° gives β = 31.2°).
