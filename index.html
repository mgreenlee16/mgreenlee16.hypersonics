Compressible aerodynamics

Compressible Flow Lab

Interactive lessons and calculators for compressible aerodynamics. Each lesson pairs the derivations with figures computed live from the exact relations for a calorically perfect gas. Change the Mach number, the geometry or the back pressure, and the wave pattern, pressure distribution and forces update at once.

The lessons build on one another. Part I covers one-dimensional flow, Part II turns to two-dimensional supersonic waves, and Part III applies both to airfoils from high subsonic to supersonic speeds.

Notes

Using the site

The ratio of specific heats, γ, in the top bar of each lesson and calculator applies to every figure and value on that page, and is remembered between pages. Theme and text size are remembered too.

Everything assumes a calorically perfect gas (constant γ and cp). That is accurate for air up to stagnation temperatures of roughly 800–1000 K. Corrections and suggestions are welcome through the repository's issue tracker.
