About this section

How the questions are made

The problems are original. They are modeled on the style and format of the course's homework and exams, but none is copied: numerical problems use new numbers each time, computed from the same relations as the rest of this site (and checked by tests/validate-334.js, which reproduces the published answers of a past exam). The qualitative questions follow the course's learning objectives and knowledge checks. The official scope, equation sheet and tables are always those given by the instructors.
